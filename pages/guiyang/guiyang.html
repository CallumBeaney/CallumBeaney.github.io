關於我頁面

I decided to take a cheap keyring camera with me to Guiyang. I decided roughly 10 minutes after arriving that I would shoot the whole trip with it, using only the four terrible-quality colour filters. It makes people look strange; the people shown are not.

I made a selection by removing ones that didn't work in the overall sequence, and had a print shop print the colours, lovely & rich in person, on some nice paper, with an exposed spine binding. This is just a short selection of the spreads, and it's just an "edition" of 1. If you want to see more, or you want your own copy, get in touch.
